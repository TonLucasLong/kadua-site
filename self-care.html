Self-care, the practical kind

Short guides you can read in five minutes and use today. They cover everyday wellbeing and how to use support well. They are information, not diagnosis or treatment, and they never replace a professional.

  • Before you call a hotlineWhat the call is like, what to have ready, and what to do if you can't get through.
  • Everyday self-care that costs nothingSleep, movement, daylight, routine, and people: small moves with evidence behind them.
  • How to support someone who's strugglingListening without fixing, asking the hard question, and knowing when to call.

How these guides are written

Each guide is drafted from public health guidance and cited where it makes a factual claim. Before a guide is marked reviewed, a qualified mental health adviser reads it and signs off. Guides that haven't been reviewed yet say so at the top. We update them when the sources change, and the review date is shown on every page.
