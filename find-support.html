    How listings are verified

    Five steps before a name appears, and a recheck on a schedule after.

    1. Application

      A professional or organization submits their details and license number.

    2. License and contact check

      The license is checked on the Professional Regulation Commission's online verification; the phone, email, or booking link is confirmed to reach them; fees are confirmed current.

    3. Adviser spot-check and recheck

      A licensed adviser sets the verification policy and spot-checks at least one in five listings each month. Every listing is rechecked on a schedule and carries its last-checked date; one that fails comes down until fixed.

    Kadua lists; it does not endorse or supervise care. Your relationship is with the professional you choose.
