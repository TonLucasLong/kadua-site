    How listings are verified

    1. Application A professional or organization submits their details and license number.
    2. License check Kadua checks the license against the Professional Regulation Commission's public verification, or the organization's registration.
    3. Contact check We confirm the phone, email, or booking link actually reaches them, and that the fee information is current.
    4. Review by a qualified adviser A licensed adviser reviews the listing before it goes live.
    5. Re-verification Every listing is rechecked on a schedule and carries its last-checked date. Listings that fail a recheck come down until fixed.

    Kadua lists; it does not endorse or supervise care. Your relationship is with the professional you choose.
