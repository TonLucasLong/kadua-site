Ano ang kailangan mo ngayon?

What do you need right now? Kadua means someone who is with you. Pick one of the four doors below and we'll point you to a real next step: a professional, a guide, a group, or a line you can call tonight.

Kadua doesn't diagnose, treat, or replace a professional. It helps you find the right one faster.

  • Professional SupportPropesyonal na tulong

    A directory of psychologists, psychiatrists, and counselors you can search and filter by type, mode, area, and fee range. Every listing goes through a verification process before it appears.
  • Self-CarePag-aalaga sa sarili

    Short, practical guides on everyday wellbeing and on how to use support well. Information, not treatment, and never a replacement for it.
  • Community & HobbiesKomunidad at libangan

    Curated hobby and interest groups, community organizations, and events where you can meet people, online or in person, around something you enjoy.
  • Immediate HelpAgarang tulong

    Verified crisis and emergency lines, checked regularly, with a plain explanation of what happens when you call.

What Kadua is, and isn't

Support can mean more than one thing. Some days you need reliable information. Some days you need a professional, or an everyday habit, or people, or urgent help. Those resources exist, but they live on different websites, directories, and pages, and finding the right one when you're already tired is its own problem.

Kadua puts the starting points in one place and tells you plainly what each one is for. It is not a clinic, a hotline, or a substitute for qualified care. Think of it as the friend who knows where to look.

How it works

  1. Discover You find Kadua through search, a friend, a school, a workplace, or a partner group.
  2. Choose a need Pick a door: professional support, self-care, community, or immediate help.
  3. Explore Get a short, categorized list with a clear next step for each option.
  4. Connect Follow through to the provider, guide, group, or hotline. Kadua steps back here.
  5. Return and tell us Come back when you need another door, and tell us what was missing so the next person finds it faster.
