Kung may panganib ngayon, tumawag sa 911.

If you or someone with you is in danger right now, call 911 or go to the nearest hospital emergency room. Kadua is not an emergency service. The lines below are.

Call 911 national emergency, 24/7

NCMH Crisis Hotline

Run by the National Center for Mental Health under the Department of Health. Open 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays. Free from landlines on the toll-free number.

1553 toll-free landline 0919-057-1553 mobile 0917-899-8727 mobile

Numbers last checked on 2026-10-25. If a number doesn't connect, try the next one, then 911.

In Touch Community Services Crisis Line

A non-profit crisis line, open 24 hours a day. Numbers confirmed on the organization's own site.

(02) 8893-7603 landline 0917-800-1123 mobile 0919-056-0709 mobile 0917-108-5412 mobile

What happens when you call

  • A responder answers. Speak in whatever language is easier for you.
  • You don't need the right words. "Hindi ko alam kung paano magsimula" is a fine first sentence.
  • Expect to be asked what's happening and whether you're safe right now. Answer as honestly as you can.
  • They will talk with you about what to do next and where to get help near you.
  • Give only what you're comfortable giving.

If the line is busy, other people are calling too. Try again, try the next number, or ask someone near you to stay with you while you do.

If you can't call

  • Tell one person near you, in person or by message, that you're not okay and you need them to stay with you.
  • Go to the emergency room of the nearest hospital. You don't need an appointment.
  • Put sharp objects, medicines, or anything you could use to hurt yourself out of reach, or ask someone to hold them for now.

For someone else

Stay with them, listen without arguing or diagnosing, ask directly whether they're thinking of hurting themselves, and call a line above together. Our guide How to support someone who's struggling has more.

How we keep this page right

Every number here is checked against the operating organization's own page or a dated news report before it goes live, and rechecked every 90 days. Lines we couldn't confirm are kept off this page rather than listed with a guess. Report a wrong or missing number.

Sources checked on 2026-10-25: Daily Tribune and DZRH reports of 3 January 2026 for the NCMH lines; in-touch.org for the In Touch lines; DILG Unified 911 launch reports of September 2025 for 911. Full record in the project's verification log.
