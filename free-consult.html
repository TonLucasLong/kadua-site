    Before your first visit

    Three things that make a free consult go smoother.

    1. Bring an ID

      A valid ID, and proof of address if the service is free for city residents. Bring your PhilHealth number or ID if the entry says free with PhilHealth.

    2. Ask about the line

      Government outpatient clinics often give numbers early in the morning. Call to ask what time to come and whether you need a referral from your barangay health center first.

    3. Ask about free medicines

      The consult is free, but medicines and tests may not be. Health centers in Manila and Pasig give some mental health medicines for free with a prescription, and Quezon City health centers do too for residents.

    Have PhilHealth?

    PhilHealth's outpatient mental health package covers members and dependents aged 10 and up: up to PHP 9,000 a year for general mental health care and up to PHP 16,000 for specialty care, at PhilHealth-accredited facilities. It covers assessment, follow-up consults, tests, and psychosocial support. Ask the facility first whether it is accredited for the package and whether you'll pay anything on top. Source: PhilHealth, 5 March 2025.

    How we keep this list right

    An entry goes on this page only when the hospital, city government, or clinic's own page, a government agency, or a dated report from a major news outlet confirms that the consultation is free and says who it's free for. Anything we couldn't confirm stays off. Every entry is rechecked every 90 days.

    Kadua lists these services; it doesn't run them or speak for them. Report a wrong detail or a free clinic we missed.
