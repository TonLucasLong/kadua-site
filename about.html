About Kadua

Kadua means a companion, someone who is with you. The tagline, Kasama sa Bawat Hakbang, says what the site is for: helping you find the right resource, professional, community, or urgent-help line for the situation you're in, one step at a time.

Four doors, one starting point

  • Professional Support. A searchable directory of psychologists, psychiatrists, counselors, and organizations. Each listing is verified before it appears; the directory opens with sample entries until the first real ones clear.
  • Self-Care. Plain-language guides, and later a small line of simple self-care items.
  • Community and Hobbies. Curated groups and events, with participation rules and a report channel.
  • Immediate Help. Verified crisis and emergency lines, always one tap away on every page.

What Kadua is not

  • Not a clinic, and not a provider of therapy or medication.
  • Not an emergency service. In danger, call 911 or the NCMH Crisis Hotline.
  • Not a diagnosis. Nothing here tells you what condition you have.
  • Not a promise. We don't guarantee outcomes, and no product we might sell replaces professional treatment.

Who does what

  • Project management. Strategy, partnerships, budget, quality, day-to-day operations.
  • Technology and platform. The website, hosting, security, maintenance, technical support.
  • Content and professional verification. Reviews every guide, verifies every listing, works with qualified advisers.
  • Marketing and customer relations. Brand, social media, feedback, partner relations.
  • Community and safety moderation. Rules, reports, organizer verification, escalation.
  • Product and supply. Suppliers, inventory, packaging, fulfillment, when Phase 2 opens.

Your information

This site has no accounts and stores nothing about you. It doesn't ask who you are to show you a hotline or a guide. If you fill in one of our forms, we collect only what the form asks for, say what it's for, keep it only as long as that purpose needs, and delete it when you ask. We never sell or share it. Kadua follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and treats anything about your health as sensitive by default.

Questions about your data, or want something deleted? Contact details will appear here once set up.

Tell us what's missing

Kadua gets better when people say what didn't work. A dead link, a wrong number, a guide that didn't help, a group that should be listed: send it here.
