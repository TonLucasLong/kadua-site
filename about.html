About Kadua

Kadua means a companion, someone who is with you. The tagline, Kasama sa Bawat Hakbang, says what the site is for: helping you find the right resource, professional, community, or urgent-help line for the situation you're in, one step at a time.

Four doors, one starting point

  • Professional Support. A searchable directory of psychologists, psychiatrists, counselors, and organizations. Each listing will be checked (license, contact, adviser spot-check) before it appears; the directory opens with labelled sample entries until the first real ones clear.
  • Self-Care. Plain-language guides, and later a small line of simple self-care items.
  • Community and Hobbies. Curated groups and events, with participation rules and a report channel.
  • Immediate Help. Confirmed crisis and emergency lines, always one tap away on every page.

What Kadua is not

  • Not a clinic, and not a provider of therapy or medication.
  • Not an emergency service. In danger, call 911 or the NCMH Crisis Hotline.
  • Not a diagnosis. Nothing here tells you what condition you have.
  • Not a promise. We don't guarantee outcomes, and no product we might sell replaces professional treatment.

The plan: start small, validate, improve, expand

Each phase opens only when the evidence gate before it is met.

  1. Now1. Foundation

    This website: resource center, professional directory, Immediate Help hub. Gate: people use it, listings and content stay verified, costs stay manageable.

  2. 2. Self-Care

    A small, tested self-care product line. Gate: real demand, workable margins, reliable delivery, good feedback.

  3. 3. Connect

    Hobby and community partnerships, pilot activities. Gate: participation, partner interest, moderation capacity, safety.

  4. 4. Expand

    One integrated mobile app. Gate: sustained demand, funding, technical and operational readiness.

Who does what

  • Project lead. Partnerships, budget, quality, day-to-day operations.
  • Content and verification. Every guide reviewed, every listing verified, hotlines rechecked, with a licensed mental health adviser.
  • Technology. The website, hosting, security, and support.
  • Marketing and community. Partner organizers, social channels, feedback, and the moderation contact who covers evenings and weekends.

Your information

The site itself has no accounts and stores nothing about you. It doesn't ask who you are to show you a hotline or a guide, and fonts and photos are served from this site, not from third parties. This study build runs no analytics at all. When the pilot launches, the site will count visits in aggregate only, with no cookies and nothing that identifies you, and this page will say so on the day it starts. The only information Kadua holds arrives through its forms (listing applications, interest forms, reports), which run on a form provider under a privacy notice and your consent; we collect only what the form asks for, say what it's for, keep it only as long as that purpose needs, and delete it when you ask. We never sell or share it. Kadua follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and treats anything about your health as sensitive by default.

Questions about your data, or want something deleted? Contact details will appear here once set up.

Tell us what's missing

Kadua gets better when people say what didn't work. A dead link, a wrong number, a guide that didn't help, a group that should be listed: send it here.
