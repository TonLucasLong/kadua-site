Community and hobbies

Feeling connected is part of wellbeing, and it's easier to connect around something you enjoy. Kadua curates hobby and interest groups, community organizations, and events, and shows you how to join. Groups are listed only after we've talked to the organizers and agreed on the rules below.

Partner listings are being set up. The categories below show what the community section will hold. Each category gets real groups as partner organizers come on board and agree to the participation rules.

    Participation rules

    • Organizers are known to us. Every listed group has a named organizer we've spoken with and a way to reach them.
    • Be kind, stay safe. No harassment, no sharing of other people's private information, no medical advice from members to members.
    • Report anything. Every listing has a report link. Reports go to Kadua's moderation contact and are acted on, with the organizer looped in.
    • Peer support is not therapy. Support groups listed here are run by partner organizations with their own moderators. If someone is in crisis, the group points to the Immediate Help lines.
    • Public first. Meetings happen in public places or on public platforms, and new members are told what to expect before they join.
