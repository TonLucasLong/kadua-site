What to expect in a first session

Information, not diagnosis or treatment. This guide was drafted from the sources listed at the end and has not yet been reviewed by a qualified mental health adviser. Review status: pending. Last updated 2026-10-25. If you are in danger right now, go to Immediate Help.

The first session is mostly a conversation about what brought you in, not a test you can fail. Knowing the shape of it makes it easier to show up.

Before

  • Write three lines: what's been happening, how long, and what you'd like to be different. Bring it. Reading it out is allowed.
  • Note anything practical: medicines you take, big life events this year, sleep lately.
  • Ask about fees, length (usually 45 to 60 minutes), online or in person, and cancellation rules before you book, so money isn't a surprise.

During

  • Expect questions about your history, your day-to-day, your supports, and your safety. "Have you had thoughts of hurting yourself?" is a standard question, asked to help, not to judge.
  • You set the pace. You can say "I'm not ready to talk about that yet."
  • Confidentiality has limits: if there's a serious risk to your life or someone else's, the professional has a duty to act. A good one explains this up front.
  • Toward the end, ask: "What do you think is going on, and what would working together look like?"

After

  • It's normal to feel tired, lighter, or a bit raw. Plan something gentle after.
  • Fit matters. If after two or three sessions you don't feel heard, it's fine to say so or to try someone else. That's not failure; that's finding the right person.

If it's online

Find a private spot, use earphones, and tell the household you need the room for an hour. Check the platform works a day before.

Sources: Republic Act No. 11036, Mental Health Act of 2018 (rights of service users, including confidentiality and informed consent); World Health Organization. (2020). Doing what matters in times of stress: An illustrated guide (https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240003927).
